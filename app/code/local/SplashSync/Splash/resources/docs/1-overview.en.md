ERP, CRM, other online stores: each of your applications holds a piece of your business.
The Splash module turns your **Magento 1 store into a sales channel connected** to all these
applications, and keeps your data continuously in sync, with no double entry and no manual export. :rocket:

### Why Splash for Magento 1?

#### :shopping_cart: All your sales in one place

Orders and invoices from your Magento store are automatically exported to your ERP, your management
software or any other connected application. All your sales are gathered in a single place,
whatever the channel.

#### :package: Accurate stocks, everywhere

Stocks are critical for your business! A stock updated in your ERP is updated in Magento too: no
more orders for out-of-stock products.

#### :busts_in_silhouette: One customer record, always up to date

With the Splash Linker, the profiles of a same customer spread across your applications are
identified and merged. A change made in one place is applied everywhere, from the CRM to the
store.

#### :bar_chart: Financial management that fills itself

Orders, invoices and credit notes are automatically exported to your management applications.
Your financial follow-up becomes simpler... and effortless.

#### :inbox_tray: Magento as your main database?

The module can also import into Magento the customers and orders coming from your other online
stores (Prestashop, WooCommerce...). Simply dedicate a Magento website to collecting this data.

### What gets synchronized

| Magento object | What is supported |
|---|---|
| Customers | Identity, email, company, date of birth, newsletter subscription |
| Addresses | Billing and shipping addresses, phone, VAT number |
| Products | Catalog, SKU, prices, stocks, images, descriptions, SEO, translations |
| Orders | Lines, statuses, addresses, payments, carrier and tracking number |
| Invoices | Lines, totals excl. and incl. tax, statuses (read only) |
| Credit notes | Refund lines and totals (read only) |

### Built for real-life use

- :globe_with_meridians: **Multilingual**: each store view is bound to a language, and product
  labels and descriptions are synchronized in all your languages.
- :office: **Multi-website**: choose the default website that will receive customers and products
  created from your other applications.
- :receipt: **Prices under control**: product prices excluding or including tax, according to your
  store configuration.
- :gear: **Fine-grained settings**: default attribute set and warehouse, expert mode for advanced
  setups.

### Compatibility

- Magento **1.9**
- PHP **7.2** or later
- An active Splash Sync account
